--- HUNZA VALLEY ---
Location: Gilgit-Baltistan, Northern Pakistan (2,438m altitude)
Province: Gilgit-Baltistan
Type: Mountain Valley, Nature, Adventure, Cultural

Description:
Hunza Valley is one of Pakistan's most breathtaking destinations, surrounded
by the mighty Karakoram mountain range. Known as "Heaven on Earth", it offers
stunning views of Rakaposhi (7,788m), Ultar Sar, and Lady Finger peaks.
The valley is home to the ancient Hunzakut people with a rich culture and
history dating back thousands of years.

Best Season to Visit: April to October
- April-May: Cherry blossom season (most photogenic)
- June-August: Peak summer, all roads open
- September-October: Autumn colors, harvest season
- November-March: Cold and snowy, some roads may close

Estimated Budget (per person, 5 days):
- Budget: PKR 15,000-20,000 (local guesthouses, local food)
- Mid-range: PKR 25,000-35,000 (decent hotels, some activities)
- Luxury: PKR 50,000-80,000 (Serena Karimabad, premium experiences)

How to Reach:
- By Air: Gilgit Airport from Islamabad (PIA, 45 min flight, weather dependent)
- By Road: Karakoram Highway (KKH) from Islamabad — 16-18 hours drive
  Beautiful but long route via Abbottabad, Besham, Chilas, Gilgit

Must Visit Attractions:
1. Baltit Fort (1,000 years old, UNESCO World Heritage Site candidate)
2. Altit Fort (3,000 years old, oldest fort in Hunza)
3. Attabad Lake (turquoise blue lake, boat rides available)
4. Eagle's Nest Viewpoint (panoramic view of entire valley)
5. Duikar (top of Hunza, sunrise point)
6. Rakaposhi Base Camp Trek
7. Passu Cones (dramatic mountain landscape)
8. Borith Lake
9. Khunjerab Pass (4,693m, Pakistan-China border, world's highest paved border)
10. Ganish Village (oldest settlement in Hunza)

Local Food & Restaurants:
- Chapshuro: Traditional Hunzakut meat-filled pastry (must try!)
- Diram Phiti: Hunza bread with apricot oil
- Mamtu: Hunza-style dumplings (similar to Chinese momos)
- Harissa: Wheat and meat porridge
- Hunza Water: Famous spring water, said to be reason for longevity
- Dried Apricots: Hunza is famous for organic apricots worldwide
- Walnut and Mulberry products

Recommended Restaurants in Hunza:
1. Hidden Paradise Restaurant, Karimabad (local food, rooftop view)
2. Cafe de Hunza, Karimabad (tourist friendly, good variety)
3. Old Hunza Restaurant (traditional recipes)
4. Marco Polo Restaurant, Karimabad

Hotels & Accommodation:
Luxury:
- Serena Hotel Karimabad (best in Hunza, mountain views, PKR 15,000-25,000/night)
- Hunza Serena Inn

Mid-range:
- Eagle's Nest Hotel (famous viewpoint hotel, PKR 5,000-8,000/night)
- Six Senses Hunza (boutique, PKR 8,000-12,000/night)
- Diran Guest House

Budget:
- PTDC Motel Karimabad (government run, PKR 2,000-4,000/night)
- Old Hunza Inn (PKR 1,500-3,000/night)
- Multiple local guesthouses (PKR 800-2,000/night)

Travel Tips:
- Always check weather forecast before traveling KKH
- Carry warm clothes even in summer (nights are cold)
- Altitude sickness possible — rest on arrival day
- Local SIM works but internet is slow
- Carry cash — ATMs limited in remote areas
- Respect local Ismaili Muslim customs
- Cherry blossom festival (March-April) is extremely popular — book in advance

--- SKARDU ---
Location: Gilgit-Baltistan, Baltistan region (2,228m)
Type: Adventure, Trekking, Lakes, Desert

Description:
Skardu is the gateway to some of the world's highest mountains including K2
(8,611m — world's second highest peak). It features a unique combination of
cold desert, blue lakes, and soaring peaks. Known as the "City of Lakes".

Best Season: June to September
Estimated Budget (5 days): PKR 20,000-40,000 per person

Must Visit:
1. Shangrila Resort Lake (Lower Kachura Lake) — fairytale setting
2. Upper Kachura Lake (Satpara)
3. Deosai National Park (world's second highest plateau, June-September)
   - Home to Himalayan brown bears
4. Shigar Fort (400 year old fort, now a heritage hotel)
5. Khaplu Palace and Ghanche
6. K2 Base Camp Trek (2-3 weeks, for experienced trekkers)
7. Concordia — junction of world's greatest glaciers

How to Reach:
- By Air: Skardu Airport from Islamabad (PIA, weather dependent)
- By Road: Via Gilgit — 4-5 hours, or Karakoram Highway — 24+ hours

Famous Hotels:
- Shangrila Resort (iconic, on lake, PKR 8,000-15,000/night)
- Shigar Fort Residence (heritage hotel inside actual fort)
- PTDC Motel Skardu
- Al-Noor Hotel Skardu

--- SWAT VALLEY ---
Location: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Province
Also Known As: Switzerland of Pakistan, Yousafzai homeland

Description:
Swat Valley is a lush green paradise with rivers, waterfalls, meadows, and
historical Buddhist sites. Once a popular tourist destination, Swat has fully
revived its tourism and is safer and more beautiful than ever.

Best Season: April to October
Estimated Budget (4 days): PKR 12,000-25,000 per person

Must Visit:
1. Mingora (main city, shopping, Swat Museum)
2. Malam Jabba Ski Resort (skiing in winter, adventure sports in summer)
3. Kalam Valley (upper Swat, stunning beauty)
4. Mahodand Lake
5. Ushu Forest
6. Buddhist Archaeological Sites (Butkara, Nimogram)
7. Madyan town
8. Bahrain (famous tourist town on river)
9. Fizagat Park

How to Reach:
- By Road from Islamabad: 5-6 hours via Motorway M-1 and Malakand

Famous Hotels:
- Malam Jabba Resort Hotel
- Rock City Hotel, Kalam
- Swat Serena Hotel, Saidu Sharif
- Multiple guesthouses in Kalam (PKR 1,500-4,000/night)

Food Specialties:
- Swati Karahi (white karahi, specialty)
- Chapli Kebab (better than Peshawar version some say!)
- Fresh river trout fish

--- LAHORE ---
Location: Capital of Punjab Province
Also Known As: Heart of Pakistan, City of Gardens, Cultural Capital

Description:
Lahore is Pakistan's most culturally rich city with over 1,000 years of
Mughal history. It is famous for its food, architecture, music, and
festivals. Lahore is a must-visit for every tourist to Pakistan.

Best Season: October to March (avoid April-June: extremely hot, 40-48°C)
Estimated Budget (3-4 days):
- Budget: PKR 8,000-12,000
- Mid-range: PKR 15,000-25,000
- Luxury: PKR 30,000-60,000+

How to Reach:
- By Air: Allama Iqbal International Airport (international flights available)
- By Train: Main Lahore Railway Station (from Karachi, Islamabad, Peshawar)
- By Road: M-2 Motorway from Islamabad (3.5 hours)

Must Visit Attractions:
1. Badshahi Mosque (one of world's largest mosques, Mughal era)
2. Lahore Fort / Shahi Qila (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
3. Shalimar Gardens (UNESCO World Heritage Site, Mughal garden)
4. Walled City of Lahore (Delhi Gate, Wazir Khan Mosque, Heera Mandi)
5. Minar-e-Pakistan (monument to Pakistan Resolution 1940)
6. Lahore Museum (one of oldest museums in subcontinent)
7. Anarkali Bazaar (oldest bazaar in Pakistan)
8. Liberty Market (modern shopping)
9. Gulshan-e-Iqbal Park
10. Wagah Border Ceremony (daily flag-lowering ceremony, Pakistan-India)

Famous Food Street & Restaurants:
Lahore Food Street (Fort Road Food Street):
- Open evenings, outdoor dining with view of Badshahi Mosque
- Best experience for tourists

Must-Eat Foods in Lahore:
- Nihari: Slow-cooked beef stew, best at Waris Nihari (60+ year old shop)
- Paye: Trotters curry, traditionally eaten for breakfast
- Lahori Chargha: Whole fried chicken, crispy and spicy
- Halwa Puri: Traditional Pakistani breakfast (fried bread with semolina pudding)
- Siri Paye: Brain and trotters, local specialty
- Kulfi Faluda: Ice cream dessert, Anarkali Bazaar
- Dahi Bhalle: Yogurt-based snack

Famous Restaurants:
1. Cuckoo's Den (Heera Mandi, rooftop, Mughal view, great ambiance)
2. Salt'n Pepper Village (best traditional Pakistani food)
3. Cooco's Den (Fort Road, outdoor seating)
4. Waris Nihari (famous for over 60 years)
5. Butt Karahi (Burns Road, Karahi specialist)
6. Phajja Siri Paye (Burns Road, famous since 1947)
7. Haveli Restaurant (Fort Road, live music, great view)
8. Cafe Zouk (modern cafe, Mall Road area)

Famous Bazaars / Shopping:
- Anarkali Bazaar: Clothes, traditional items, affordable
- Ichhra Bazaar: Clothes wholesale
- Hafeez Centre: Electronics
- Gulberg: Upscale shopping, international brands
- Landa Bazaar: Second-hand items, unique finds

Hotels:
Luxury:
- Pearl Continental Lahore (5-star, PKR 20,000-40,000/night)
- Avari Hotel (5-star, PKR 15,000-30,000/night)
- Faletti's Hotel (heritage hotel, 1880)

Mid-range:
- Hotel One Lahore (PKR 5,000-10,000/night)
- Crown Plaza Lahore
- Regent Plaza Hotel

Budget:
- Punjab House (government rest house, limited rooms)
- Multiple hotels near Railway Station (PKR 1,500-4,000/night)
- Airbnb options available

--- ISLAMABAD ---
Location: Federal Capital of Pakistan
Type: Modern city, Nature, Day trips

Description:
Pakistan's modern capital, built in the 1960s, is a planned city famous for
its cleanliness, greenery, and proximity to nature. Surrounded by Margalla
Hills, it is one of the greenest capitals in the world.

Best Season: October to April
Estimated Budget (2-3 days): PKR 10,000-20,000 per person

Must Visit:
1. Faisal Mosque (one of world's largest mosques, iconic architecture)
2. Pakistan Monument (lotus-shaped monument, Pakistani culture museum)
3. Margalla Hills National Park (hiking trails, wildlife)
4. Daman-e-Koh (viewpoint, view of entire Islamabad)
5. Shakarparian National Park
6. Lok Virsa Museum (Pakistan folk heritage)
7. Centaurus Mall (modern shopping)
8. F-6 Supermarket (Sunday market)
9. Saidpur Village (historic village turned arts village)
10. Lake View Park

Day Trips from Islamabad:
- Taxila Museum (archaeological site, 30 min drive)
- Rohtas Fort, Jhelum (1.5 hour drive)
- Murree Hill Station (1 hour drive)
- Attock Fort

Famous Restaurants in Islamabad:
1. Monal Restaurant (on Margalla Hills, best view of Islamabad)
2. Kokonor (Islamabad's best Chinese)
3. Tuscany Courtyard (Italian)
4. Saidpur Village Restaurant (traditional setting)
5. Savour Foods (Jinnah Super, famous Pakistani BBQ)

Hotels:
- Serena Hotel Islamabad (5-star, PKR 25,000-50,000/night)
- Marriott Hotel Islamabad (5-star)
- Hotel One Islamabad (mid-range, PKR 6,000-12,000/night)
- Shelton's Rezidor Hotel

--- MURREE ---
Location: Rawalpindi District, Punjab (2,290m)
Type: Hill Station, Family Tourism, Winter Destination

Description:
Pakistan's most popular hill station, just 1 hour from Islamabad. Famous for
its colonial-era architecture, pine forests, and snowfall in winter.

Best Season: 
- Summer (May-September): Escape Islamabad heat

Must Visit:
1. Mall Road (main bazaar, food, shopping)
2. Pindi Point (chairlift, great views)
3. Kashmir Point
4. GPO Chowk
5. Pipeline Track (hiking through pine forests)
6. Patriata / New Murree (chairlift, snowfall area)

Hotels: Many options along Mall Road
- Lockwood Hotel (heritage, PKR 5,000-10,000/night)
- Hotel One Murree
- Multiple guesthouses (PKR 2,000-5,000/night)

--- MULTAN ---
Location: Southern Punjab
Also Known As: City of Saints, City of Shrines, Mango Capital

Description:
Multan is one of the oldest cities in the world (over 5,000 years old).
Famous for shrines of Sufi saints, blue pottery, and mangoes.
An authentic Pakistani cultural experience away from tourist crowds.

Best Season: October to February
Estimated Budget: PKR 6,000-12,000 per person (3 days)

Must Visit:
1. Shah Rukn-e-Alam Shrine (gorgeous Mughal-era shrine)
2. Bahauddin Zakariya Shrine
3. Multan Fort
4. Ghanta Ghar (Clock Tower, city center)
5. Blue Pottery workshops (buy authentic blue pottery)
6. Multan Museum

Famous Food:
- Sohan Halwa: Famous sweet, PKR 800-1,500/kg
- Multani Biryani (different from Karachi style)
- Dhal Baati (Multan specialty)
- Fresh Mangoes (summer season)

--- KARACHI ---
Location: Capital of Sindh Province, Largest City of Pakistan
Type: Metropolitan, Coastal, Food, Business

Description:
Karachi is Pakistan's economic hub and largest city with a population of
over 20 million. It is a cosmopolitan city with beautiful beaches, amazing
street food, historic sites, and a vibrant culture. Famous for being the
most diverse city in Pakistan.

Best Season: November to February (avoid March-August: hot and humid)
Estimated Budget (3 days):
- Budget: PKR 8,000-15,000
- Mid-range: PKR 15,000-30,000

Must Visit:
1. Clifton Beach (Sea View) — most popular beach
2. French Beach — cleaner, more scenic
3. Hawks Bay — great for swimming
4. Quaid's Mausoleum (tomb of Pakistan's founder, Muhammad Ali Jinnah)
5. Pakistan Maritime Museum
6. Mohatta Palace Museum
7. National Museum of Pakistan
8. Empress Market (colonial-era market, 1889)
9. Burns Road Food Street (most famous food street)
10. Dolmen Mall Clifton (upscale shopping)

Famous Food in Karachi:
- Karachi Biryani: Pakistan's most famous rice dish — spicy, flavorful
  Best at: Student Biryani, Burns Road Biryani centers
- Bun Kebab: Karachi's street food king — spiced beef patty in bun
  Try at: Burns Road, Stadium Road
- Karachi BBQ: Mixed grill platter
- Seekh Kebab: Minced meat on skewers
- Nihari: Also popular in Karachi
- Haleem: Slow-cooked meat and lentil stew
- Rabri Faluda: Cold dessert
- Lassi: Yogurt drink

Famous Restaurants:
1. Do Darya (waterfront restaurants, multiple options, Clifton)
2. Burns Road (entire street of historic restaurants)
3. Village Restaurant (traditional Pakistani)
4. Kolachi (Do Darya area, seafood and Pakistani)
5. Boat Basin (F-10, many restaurant options)
6. Koel Cafe (trendy, modern)
7. Cafe Flo (fine dining)
8. BBQ Tonight (Burns Road, famous for grills)

Hotels:
Luxury:
- Pearl Continental Karachi (5-star, PKR 20,000-45,000/night)
- Movenpick Hotel (5-star, sea view)
- Avari Towers Karachi

Mid-range:
- Best Western (multiple locations)
- Hotel Mehran (PKR 6,000-12,000/night)

--- MOHENJO-DARO ---
Location: Larkana District, Sindh
Type: Archaeological, UNESCO World Heritage Site

Description:
One of the world's oldest and largest urban settlements (2500-1500 BCE).
The ancient Indus Valley Civilization site is 5,000 years old.
A must-visit for history and archaeology lovers.

Best Season: October to March
How to Reach: Flight to Mohenjo-Daro airport from Karachi, or train to Larkana

Must See:
- The Great Bath (ancient public bathing structure)
- Granary and Assembly Hall
- Mohenjo-Daro Museum (artifacts and excavated items)
- Worker's Quarters
- Priest King statue (original in National Museum Karachi)

- Dried Fruits: Cheapest in Pakistan here

Famous Restaurants:
- Lal's Restaurant (famous for Sajji)

--- PESHAWAR ---
Location: Capital of KPK Province
Also Known As: City of Flowers, Gateway to Central Asia

Description:
Peshawar is one of the oldest cities in the world (over 2,000 years old).
A Pashtun cultural hub with amazing food, historic bazaars, and rich culture.
Gateway to the Khyber Pass and Afghanistan.

Best Season: October to April
Estimated Budget (3 days): PKR 8,000-15,000

Must Visit:
1. Qissa Khwani Bazaar (Storyteller's Bazaar — most famous bazaar)
2. Bala Hisar Fort (imposing fort, still used by military)
3. Peshawar Museum (Gandhara Buddhist artifacts)
4. Mahabat Khan Mosque (Mughal-era mosque, 17th century)
5. Cunningham Clock Tower
6. Sethi House (heritage mansion)
7. Khyber Pass (30km from Peshawar, requires permit)

Famous Food in Peshawar — BEST FOOD IN PAKISTAN (many would argue):
- Chapli Kebab: Flat minced meat patty, specialty of KPK
  Best at: Charsi Tikka, Namak Mandi area
- Peshawari Karahi: White karahi (no tomatoes), cooked in fat/butter
  Best at: Namak Mandi restaurants
- Dum Pukht: Slow-cooked meat in sealed pot
- Kabuli Pulao: Afghan-influenced rice with meat and raisins
- Peshawari Ice Cream (famous pink ice cream)
- Doda Barfi (sweet)
- Qehwa (green tea with cardamom, traditional)

Famous Restaurants:
1. Charsi Tikka (Namak Mandi) — MUST VISIT for kebabs
2. Peshawari Inn (famous Peshawari Karahi)
3. Jan's Hotel Restaurant
4. Qissa Khwani area restaurants (authentic street food)

Hotels:
- Pearl Continental Peshawar (5-star)
- Hotel One Peshawar
- Shelton Hotel

SECTION 6: CULTURAL TIPS FOR TOURISTS
================================================================

GENERAL ETIQUETTE:
- Greet with "Assalam-o-Alaikum" (Peace be upon you) — people will love it
- Pakistanis are extremely hospitable — you may be invited for tea/food
- Remove shoes before entering mosques and some homes
- Dress modestly: women should cover head at religious sites
- Men should avoid shorts in rural/conservative areas
- Ask permission before photographing people
- Bargaining is expected and normal in bazaars (start at 50% of asking price)

RAMADAN TRAVEL (dates vary each year):
- Avoid eating/drinking in public during fasting hours
- Many restaurants closed until iftar (sunset)
- Iftar time is a beautiful experience to witness
- Sehri (pre-dawn meal) is also a unique experience

RELIGIOUS CUSTOMS:
- Friday is holy day — Jumu'ah prayer time (12-2pm) many shops close
- Eid celebrations are amazing to witness as a tourist
- Religious sites: be respectful, speak softly, no photography during prayers

SAFETY TIPS:
- Pakistan is generally safe for tourists
- Northern Pakistan (Hunza, Skardu, Swat) are very safe
- Register with PTDC for treks and remote area travel
- Keep emergency contact numbers saved
- Travel in groups for remote areas
- Share your itinerary with someone at home
- Emergency: 1122 (Rescue), 1101 (Tourist Police Helpline)

MONEY & PRACTICAL:
- Currency: Pakistani Rupee (PKR)
- Cash is preferred in most places (carry enough)
- ATMs available in all major cities, limited in remote north
- Bargaining: always negotiate in bazaars
- Tipping: not mandatory but appreciated (PKR 50-200 for small services)
- Water: buy bottled water, avoid tap water

TRANSPORT:
- Rent a car with driver for northern areas (PKR 3,000-8,000/day)
- Local van/coasters between cities (cheap but slow)
- Daewoo Bus: Air-conditioned intercity bus (reliable, affordable)
- PIA/AirBlue/Serene Air for domestic flights
- Uber/Careem available in major cities

BASIC URDU PHRASES:
- Hello/Goodbye: Assalam-o-Alaikum / Khuda Hafiz
- Thank you: Shukriya / Meherbani
- Yes/No: Ji Haan / Nahi
- How much?: Kitna hai? / Kitne ka hai?
- Where is...?: ... kahan hai?
- Please help: Madad kijiye / Mujhe madad chahiye
- Good food: Bahut acha khana
- Water: Paani
- More please: Aur dijiye
- Too expensive: Bohot mehnga hai
- Delicious: Mazedaar
- Beautiful: Khubsoorat
- I am a tourist: Main tourist hoon
- Hospital: Aspatal kahan hai?
- Police: Police kahan hai?
