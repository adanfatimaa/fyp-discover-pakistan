LAHORE — HEART OF PAKISTAN
================================================================
City: Lahore
Province: Punjab
Population: 14 million (2nd largest city)
Known As: City of Gardens | Cultural Capital | Heart of Pakistan
Language: Urdu, Punjabi
Airport: Allama Iqbal International Airport (LHE)

OVERVIEW:
Lahore is Pakistan's most visited city for tourists. It has the richest
Mughal heritage in the country — over 1,000 years of continuous history.
The city is alive 24/7 with food, music, festivals, and bazaars.
UNESCO has listed two of its sites as World Heritage Sites.

CLIMATE:
- Winter (Nov-Feb): 5-20°C, pleasant, best for tourism
- Spring (Mar-Apr): 20-30°C, beautiful, gardens in bloom
- Summer (May-Sep): 35-48°C, extremely hot, avoid
- Monsoon (Jul-Sep): Humid, heavy rain

HOW TO REACH LAHORE:
By Air:
- International: Direct flights from Dubai, London, Toronto, New York
- Domestic: PIA, AirBlue, Serene Air from Karachi, Islamabad, Peshawar
By Train:
- Main Lahore Railway Station — Green Line (Karachi), Shah Zaman Express
By Road:
- M-2 Motorway from Islamabad: 3.5 hours
- M-3/M-4 from Faisalabad: 2 hours
- GT Road from Peshawar: 5-6 hours

TOURIST AREAS IN LAHORE:
1. Walled City (Old Lahore) — historical center
2. Gulberg — modern restaurants, cafes, shopping
3. DHA — upscale area, international restaurants
4. Mall Road — colonial buildings, museums, hotels
5. Johar Town — mid-range restaurants and hotels
6. Fort Road — famous food street near Badshahi Mosque

MUST VISIT (DETAILED):
1. Badshahi Mosque
   Built: 1673 by Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb
   Capacity: 100,000 worshippers
   Timing: Open all day, entry free
   Tip: Go at sunset for magical light

2. Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila)
   UNESCO World Heritage Site
   Built: Over 1,000 years, expanded by Mughals
   Highlights: Sheesh Mahal, Alamgiri Gate, Moti Masjid
   Timing: 8am-6pm | Entry: PKR 500 foreigners, PKR 30 locals

3. Shalimar Gardens
   UNESCO World Heritage Site
   Built: 1641 by Emperor Shah Jahan (same who built Taj Mahal)
   Three terraced gardens with 410 fountains
   Entry: PKR 200

4. Wazir Khan Mosque
   Built: 1635 | Most ornate mosque in Pakistan
   Famous for: Hand-painted frescoes and tile work
   Located inside Delhi Gate, Walled City
   Entry: Free

5. Minar-e-Pakistan
   Built: 1968, marks where Pakistan Resolution was passed in 1940
   Height: 60 meters
   Entry: PKR 20 | Great night view

6. Lahore Museum
   Established: 1894 | One of oldest in South Asia
   Famous for: Fasting Buddha statue, Mughal paintings, Gandhara art
   Timing: 9am-5pm, closed Friday

7. Wagah Border
   Distance: 25km from Lahore city center
   Daily flag-lowering ceremony at sunset
   Both Pakistan and India Rangers perform
   Entry: Free | Book front seats early by arriving 2 hours before

LAHORE TRANSPORT:
- Orange Line Metro Train (modern, PKR 40 flat fare)
- Uber/Careem (most convenient)
- Rickshaw (negotiate: PKR 100-300 short distance)
- Qingqi (motorcycle rickshaw, PKR 20-50)

KARACHI — CITY OF LIGHTS
================================================================
City: Karachi
Province: Sindh
Population: 20+ million (largest city of Pakistan)
Known As: City of Lights | Economic Capital | Mini Pakistan
Language: Urdu, Sindhi, Balochi, Punjabi (most diverse city)
Airport: Jinnah International Airport (KHI)

OVERVIEW:
Karachi is Pakistan's financial and commercial hub. It is the most
cosmopolitan city in Pakistan with people from every province and culture.
Famous for its beaches, amazing street food, and never-sleeping nightlife
(relative to other Pakistani cities).

CLIMATE:
- Winter (Nov-Feb): 15-25°C, perfect for tourism
- Spring (Mar-Apr): 25-35°C, warm
- Summer (May-Sep): 35-42°C, hot and humid
- Monsoon (Jul-Aug): Heavy rain, flooding possible

HOW TO REACH KARACHI:
By Air:
- International: Major hub, flights from worldwide
- Domestic: Frequent flights from Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar
By Train:
- Karachi Cantonment Station — multiple intercity trains
By Road:
- M-9 Motorway to Hyderabad, then National Highway

KEY AREAS FOR TOURISTS:
1. Clifton — upscale, sea view, restaurants, hotels
2. Defence (DHA) — best restaurants and cafes
3. Burns Road — historic food street since 1947
4. Saddar — colonial buildings, cheap hotels, markets
5. Do Darya — waterfront dining strip
6. Boat Basin — restaurants and cafes

MUST VISIT:
1. Quaid's Mausoleum (Mazar-e-Quaid)
   Tomb of Pakistan's founder, Muhammad Ali Jinnah
   Open: Daily 8am-8pm | Entry: Free
   Best time: Morning or evening (changing of guard ceremony)

2. Clifton Beach (Sea View)
   Most popular beach | Free entry
   Camel and horse rides available
   Best time: Evening (sunset)

3. French Beach
   30km from city | Cleaner, quieter than Clifton
   Good for swimming | Entry: PKR 200-500

4. Mohatta Palace
   Built: 1927, beautiful pink sandstone palace
   Now a museum of art and culture
   Timing: Tue-Sun 10am-5pm | Entry: PKR 100

5. National Museum of Pakistan
   Largest museum in Pakistan
   Highlights: Indus Valley artifacts, Quran collection
   Timing: 9am-5pm, closed Monday

6. Empress Market
   Built: 1889 during British era
   Huge covered market — meat, spices, birds, everything
   Located in Saddar area

KARACHI TRANSPORT:
- Uber/Careem (safest for tourists)
- BRT (Bus Rapid Transit) — modern, PKR 30
- Yellow Cab/Rickshaw (negotiate price first)

ISLAMABAD — THE CAPITAL
================================================================
City: Islamabad
Status: Federal Capital Territory
Population: 2 million (planned city)
Known As: Green Capital | City of Nature | Most Beautiful Capital in Asia
Language: Urdu (official)
Airport: New Islamabad International Airport (ISB)

OVERVIEW:
Built in the 1960s to serve as Pakistan's new capital, Islamabad is the
most organized and beautiful city in Pakistan. Surrounded by Margalla Hills,
it has wide tree-lined avenues, parks, and a peaceful environment very
different from other Pakistani cities.

CLIMATE:
- Winter (Nov-Feb): 2-15°C, foggy, occasional snow on hills
- Spring (Mar-Apr): 15-25°C, best time, flowers blooming
- Summer (May-Aug): 30-40°C, less hot than Punjab plains
- Monsoon (Jul-Sep): Green and lush, heavy rain

HOW TO REACH:
By Air: New Islamabad Airport — international and domestic flights
By Train: Rawalpindi Railway Station (twin city, 20min away)
By Road: M-2 from Lahore (3.5 hrs), M-1 from Peshawar (2 hrs)

SECTOR SYSTEM:
Islamabad is divided into sectors (F, G, H, I, E series)
- F-6/F-7: Best cafes, restaurants, Jinnah Super Market
- F-8/F-10: Residential, good restaurants
- Blue Area (G-7): Commercial center, offices, hotels
- G-9 Karachi Company: Popular market

MUST VISIT:
1. Faisal Mosque
   One of world's largest mosques
   Unique tent-shaped design by Turkish architect Vedat Dalokay
   Capacity: 300,000 worshippers
   Entry: Free, non-Muslim visitors welcome (with modesty)

2. Pakistan Monument
   Built: 2007, lotus flower shape represents 4 provinces + 3 territories
   Museum inside: history of Pakistan
   Timing: 9am-9pm | Entry: PKR 20

3. Daman-e-Koh
   Viewpoint in Margalla Hills
   Best view of entire Islamabad city
   Height: 700m | Free entry
   1 hour drive from city center

4. Margalla Hills National Park
   12 hiking trails from easy to difficult
   Wildlife: Leopards, foxes, monitor lizards, birds
   Trail 3: Most popular, 2-3 hours round trip
   Trail 5: Longest, reaches Pir Sohawa viewpoint

5. Lok Virsa Museum
   Pakistan folk heritage and crafts museum
   Beautiful building and gardens
   Annual Lok Mela festival (October-November)
   Timing: Tue-Sun 10am-6pm

6. Saidpur Village
   Historic Mughal-era village preserved as heritage site
   Art galleries, restaurants, craft shops
   Very unique experience — old village inside modern capital

DAY TRIPS FROM ISLAMABAD:
- Taxila (30 min): Ancient Buddhist civilization, UNESCO site
- Murree (1 hr): Hill station, pine forests
- Rohtas Fort (1.5 hr): 16th century fort, UNESCO site
- Attock Fort (1 hr): Mughal fort on Indus River

PESHAWAR — GATEWAY TO THE NORTH
================================================================
City: Peshawar
Province: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KPK)
Population: 2.5 million
Known As: City of Flowers | Gateway to Central Asia | Oldest Living City
Language: Pashto, Urdu
Airport: Bacha Khan International Airport (PEW)

OVERVIEW:
Peshawar is over 2,500 years old — one of the oldest continuously
inhabited cities in the world. It sits at the crossroads of Central Asia,
South Asia, and the Middle East. Famous for its bazaars, Pashtun culture,
and Pakistan's best food (especially kebabs and karahi).

CLIMATE:
- Winter (Nov-Feb): 5-18°C, cool, good for visiting
- Spring (Mar-Apr): 20-30°C, pleasant
- Summer (May-Sep): 35-45°C, very hot
- Best time: October to April

HOW TO REACH:
By Air: Bacha Khan Airport — flights from Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad
By Road: M-1 Motorway from Islamabad (2 hours)
By Train: Peshawar Railway Station — trains from Karachi, Lahore

MUST VISIT:
1. Qissa Khwani Bazaar
   "Bazaar of Storytellers" — most famous bazaar in KPK
   Centuries old trading center connecting Central Asia to South Asia
   Tea houses, dry fruits, traditional items
   Best visited: Morning or evening

2. Peshawar Museum
   Finest collection of Gandhara Buddhist art in the world
   Artifacts from 2nd century BCE to 7th century CE
   Timing: 9am-5pm, closed Friday | Entry: PKR 30

3. Mahabat Khan Mosque
   Built: 1670, Mughal era
   Famous for: Two-tone blue and white painted minarets
   Located in heart of Qissa Khwani Bazaar

4. Bala Hisar Fort
   Massive 17th century fort, still used by Pakistan Army
   Exterior view only (military base inside)
   Commands view over entire city

5. Khyber Pass
   30km from Peshawar, requires permit (NOC)
   Historic mountain pass used by Alexander the Great, Mughals
   Permit from Home Department KPK government

QUETTA — CITY OF FRUITS
================================================================
City: Quetta
Province: Balochistan
Population: 1.2 million
Known As: Little Paris | Fruit Garden of Pakistan
Language: Pashto, Brahui, Balochi, Urdu
Airport: Quetta International Airport (UET)

OVERVIEW:
Quetta is surrounded by mountains and known for its cool climate,
stunning natural beauty, and the best Sajji (roasted meat) in Pakistan.
It is the capital of Balochistan, Pakistan's largest province by area.

CLIMATE:
- Winter (Dec-Feb): Can go below 0°C, heavy snowfall possible
- Spring (Mar-May): 10-25°C, beautiful, orchards in bloom
- Summer (Jun-Sep): 25-35°C, mild compared to rest of Pakistan
- Best time: March to October

HOW TO REACH:
By Air: Daily flights from Karachi, Islamabad, Lahore
By Train: Quetta Express from Karachi (24 hours)
By Road: Long drives through Balochistan (check security advisories)

FAMOUS FOR:
- Sajji: Best in all Pakistan — must try before leaving
- Dried Fruits: Cheapest prices in Pakistan (buy kilos to take home)
- Apples, Grapes, Pomegranates from Balochistan orchards

MULTAN — CITY OF SAINTS
================================================================
City: Multan
Province: South Punjab
Population: 2 million
Known As: City of Saints | City of Shrines | Mango Capital
Language: Saraiki, Urdu
Airport: Multan International Airport (MUX)

OVERVIEW:
Multan is one of the oldest cities in the world — over 5,000 years old.
It has the highest density of Sufi shrines in Pakistan and is famous for
its spiritual atmosphere, blue pottery craft, and mangoes.

CLIMATE:
- Winter (Nov-Feb): 5-20°C, best season
- Summer (May-Sep): 40-50°C, extremely hot (hottest major city)
- Best time: October to February

HOW TO REACH:
By Air: Flights from Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad
By Train: Several trains from Lahore, Karachi
By Road: M-4 Motorway from Lahore (3 hours)

FAMOUS SHRINES:
1. Shah Rukn-e-Alam (1324 CE) — most beautiful shrine in Pakistan
2. Bahauddin Zakariya Shrine — founder of Suhrawardiyya Sufi order
3. Shah Shams Sabzwari Shrine

BLUE POTTERY:
Multan is famous for hand-painted blue pottery — unique Pakistani craft.
Buy direct from artisans in old city workshops.
Prices: PKR 200-5,000 depending on size and complexity.

FAISALABAD — CITY OF INDUSTRY
================================================================
City: Faisalabad
Province: Punjab
Population: 4 million (3rd largest city)
Known As: Manchester of Pakistan | City of Textiles
Language: Punjabi, Urdu
Airport: Faisalabad International Airport (LYP)

OVERVIEW:
Faisalabad is Pakistan's industrial hub and textile capital. While not a
traditional tourist destination, it has a unique culture, famous clock
tower bazaars, and is worth visiting for authentic Punjab experience.

Famous For:
- Eight bazaars in shape of Union Jack around Clock Tower (Ghanta Ghar)
- Textile factories and wholesale markets
- Authentic Punjabi food and culture
- Canal system throughout the city
