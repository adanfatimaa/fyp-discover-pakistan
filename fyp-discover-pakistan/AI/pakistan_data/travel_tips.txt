DISCOVER PAKISTAN — TRAVEL TIPS & PRACTICAL GUIDE
Safety, Budget, Transport, Visas, and Everything Practical
================================================================

This guide covers all the practical information tourists need before
and during their visit to Pakistan. Pakistan is a safe and rewarding
destination — preparation makes it even better.

================================================================
SECTION 1: VISA INFORMATION
================================================================

VISA ON ARRIVAL (Available for many countries):
Pakistan offers Visa on Arrival for citizens of many countries.
Check official Government of Pakistan website for current list.
Countries included: Many European, North American, and Asian countries.

E-VISA:
Pakistan has an online e-visa system — apply before traveling.
Website: visa.nadra.gov.pk
Processing: Usually 2-7 working days
Types: Tourist visa (single/multiple entry), Business visa

VISA FEES (Approximate, subject to change):
- Tourist visa: USD 25-75 depending on country
- Multiple entry: USD 50-100

REQUIREMENTS:
- Valid passport (6 months validity minimum)
- Return flight ticket
- Hotel booking or invitation letter
- Proof of funds (bank statement)
- Travel insurance (recommended)

SPECIAL AREAS REQUIRING ADDITIONAL PERMITS:
- Khyber Pass: NOC (No Objection Certificate) from KPK Home Department
- Areas near Line of Control (Pakistan-India border): Special permit
- Some tribal areas: Check current status with PTDC

PTDC (Pakistan Tourism Development Corporation):
Official government tourism body.
Provides: Permits, tourist guides, accommodation, information.
Website: www.tourism.gov.pk
Offices in: Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Gilgit, Skardu, Peshawar

SECTION 2: SAFETY INFORMATION
================================================================

OVERALL SAFETY ASSESSMENT:
Pakistan is significantly safer than its international reputation suggests.
Most tourist areas are very safe and welcoming to foreigners.
Millions of foreign tourists visit Pakistan each year.

SAFEST AREAS FOR TOURISTS:
- Lahore: Very safe, heavily touristed, tourist police present
- Islamabad: Capital city, high security, very safe
- Hunza and Gilgit-Baltistan: Extremely safe, very tourist friendly
- Swat Valley: Fully safe, recovered completely, beautiful
- Skardu and Baltistan: Very safe mountaineering/trekking destination
- Naran/Kaghan: Safe popular tourist destination
- Murree: Safe hill station

AREAS TO CHECK CURRENT ADVISORIES:
- Balochistan (outside Quetta): Check latest advisories before traveling
- Near Afghan border areas: Avoid unless with experienced local guide
- Former tribal areas (FATA): Some areas still sensitive, check updates

PRACTICAL SAFETY TIPS:
1. Register with your country's embassy or consulate in Pakistan
2. Share your itinerary with someone at home
3. Download offline maps (Maps.me works well in Pakistan)
4. Save emergency numbers in your phone (1122, 15, 1101)
5. Travel with a reputable tour operator for remote areas
6. In northern areas: Always check road conditions before traveling
7. Winter travel to north: Some roads close October-April
8. Carry a photocopy of your passport (keep original safe)
9. Purchase travel insurance before arriving
10. Join Pakistan travel Facebook groups for real-time updates

TOURIST POLICE:
Pakistan has Tourist Police in major cities.
Helpline: 1101
They assist tourists, provide information, and handle complaints.

TRUST YOUR INSTINCTS:
If something feels wrong, leave politely.
But note: Most interactions with Pakistanis will be genuinely friendly.

SECTION 3: BUDGET PLANNING
================================================================

PAKISTAN IS VERY AFFORDABLE:
Pakistan is one of the most budget-friendly countries for tourists.
Everything from food to transport to accommodation is very cheap.

DAILY BUDGET ESTIMATES (per person per day):

BUDGET TRAVELER (backpacker):
- Accommodation: PKR 1,000-2,500 (hostel or basic guesthouse)
- Food: PKR 500-1,000 (dhabas, street food, local restaurants)
- Transport: PKR 300-800 (local buses, rickshaws)
- Attractions: PKR 200-500 (most sights are cheap or free)
- Total: PKR 2,000-5,000 per day (approx USD 7-18)

MEDIUM BUDGET (comfortable traveler):
- Accommodation: PKR 3,000-8,000 (Hotel One, mid-range hotels)
- Food: PKR 1,500-3,000 (proper restaurants)
- Transport: PKR 1,000-2,000 (Uber/Careem or rent with driver)
- Attractions + Shopping: PKR 500-2,000
- Total: PKR 6,000-15,000 per day (approx USD 21-53)

LUXURY TRAVELER:
- Accommodation: PKR 15,000-50,000 (Pearl Continental, Serena)
- Food: PKR 5,000-15,000 (hotel restaurants, top restaurants)
- Transport: PKR 5,000-15,000 (private car and driver)
- Total: PKR 25,000-80,000+ per day (approx USD 89-285)

SPECIFIC COSTS (PKR):
Food:
- Dhaba meal (full): 200-500
- Restaurant meal: 800-2,000
- Street food snack: 50-200
- Tea at dhaba: 30-80
- Biryani plate: 250-500

Accommodation:
- Hostel dorm bed: 800-1,500
- Budget hotel room: 1,500-4,000
- Mid-range hotel: 4,000-10,000
- 5-star hotel: 15,000-50,000

Transport:
- Rickshaw (city): 100-400
- Uber/Careem short ride: 200-500
- Daewoo bus (city to city): 700-2,000
- Domestic flight (one way): 5,000-15,000
- Car with driver (day): 3,000-8,000

Attractions:
- Most mosques: Free
- Historical sites: PKR 30-500
- National parks: PKR 100-500
- Museums: PKR 30-200
- Boat rides: PKR 200-1,000

CURRENCY:
- Pakistani Rupee (PKR or Rs.)
- 1 USD ≈ 278 PKR (2024, check current rate)
- 1 EUR ≈ 305 PKR
- 1 GBP ≈ 356 PKR

MONEY TIPS:
- Carry enough cash — many places don't accept cards
- ATMs available in all major cities (limited in northern areas)
- Recommended: Exchange money at banks or official exchange shops
- Do not exchange money with street money changers
- Inform your bank before traveling (avoid card blocks)
- US Dollars and Euros easiest to exchange

SECTION 4: TRANSPORTATION
================================================================

DOMESTIC FLIGHTS:
Airlines: PIA (national carrier), AirBlue, Serene Air
Major Routes: Islamabad-Lahore-Karachi-Peshawar-Quetta-Gilgit-Skardu
Booking: Online or through travel agents
Tip: Book Gilgit and Skardu flights early — they're weather dependent
     and often cancelled in bad weather (especially winter)

TRAINS:
Pakistan Railways covers major cities.
Classes: Economy, AC Standard, AC Business (most comfortable), Sleeper
Famous Routes:
- Lahore to Karachi: Green Line (14-16 hours), Shalimar Express
- Lahore to Islamabad: 4-5 hours
- Karachi to Quetta: 18-20 hours
Book at: Railway stations or online (booking.pakrail.com)
Tip: Book AC Business class for long journeys — comfortable and affordable

BUSES:
Daewoo Express: Best intercity bus in Pakistan
- Air conditioned, comfortable, on time
- Routes: All major cities
- Booking: Online or at Daewoo terminals
- Cost: PKR 700-2,500 depending on distance

Faisal Movers, Bilal Travels: Other reliable operators
Local Coasters/Vans: Cheap but crowded, available everywhere

ROAD TRAVEL IN NORTHERN AREAS:
- Hire a jeep and driver — essential for mountain areas
- Cost: PKR 3,000-8,000 per day for jeep + driver
- Karakoram Highway (KKH): One of world's highest paved roads
- Check road conditions before traveling (landslides possible)
- NATCO buses: Government buses for Gilgit, Hunza routes

CITY TRANSPORT:
Lahore:
- Orange Line Metro: Modern train, PKR 40 flat fare
- Uber/Careem: Most convenient, safe
- Rickshaw: Negotiate fare first (PKR 100-400)

Karachi:
- BRT (Bus Rapid Transit): Modern buses, PKR 30
- Uber/Careem: Available and safe
- Yellow Cab: PKR 300-800 (negotiate)

Islamabad:
- Uber/Careem: Best option
- Metro Bus: Connects twin cities (Islamabad-Rawalpindi), PKR 30

SECTION 6: HEALTH AND MEDICAL
================================================================

VACCINATIONS (Recommended):
- Hepatitis A and B
- Typhoid
- Rabies (if trekking in remote areas)
- Yellow Fever (if coming from Yellow Fever endemic country — required)
- Routine vaccines: MMR, Tetanus, Flu

HEALTH TIPS:
- Drink only bottled or filtered water
- Avoid ice in drinks at small restaurants
- Wash hands frequently — carry hand sanitizer
- Food: Freshly cooked food at busy restaurants is generally safe
- Altitude sickness: When going to northern high altitudes, ascend slowly
  Rest on first day, stay hydrated, no alcohol
  Symptoms: Headache, nausea, breathlessness — descend if severe

MEDICAL FACILITIES:
- Major cities: Good private hospitals available
- Lahore: Aga Khan Hospital, Shaukat Khanum, Services Hospital
- Karachi: Aga Khan University Hospital, Liaquat National Hospital
- Islamabad: PIMS, Shifa International Hospital
- Northern areas: Basic facilities only — medical evacuation possible

MEDICAL INSURANCE:
STRONGLY RECOMMENDED — especially for trekking and northern areas
Get comprehensive travel insurance with medical evacuation coverage.

COMMON MEDICINES TO CARRY:
- ORS (Oral Rehydration Solution) for dehydration/diarrhea
- Imodium for stomach issues
- Antihistamine for allergies
- Sunscreen (very important in summer)
- Insect repellent (for lower elevation areas)
- Altitude sickness medication (Diamox) for northern treks

SECTION 7: CONNECTIVITY AND COMMUNICATION
================================================================

SIM CARDS:
Available at airports on arrival and at shops everywhere.
Networks: Jazz, Telenor, Ufone, Zong
Best coverage in northern areas: Jazz (also known as Mobilink)
Cost: PKR 200-500 for SIM + initial data package
Passport required for SIM registration

INTERNET:
- Major cities: Good 4G coverage
- Northern areas: Limited, slow 3G/2G in remote parts
- Hunza town: Reasonable connectivity
- Remote trekking routes: No signal
- Hotel WiFi: Available at most mid-range and above hotels

OFFLINE MAPS:
Download Maps.me with Pakistan maps before traveling.
Works completely offline — essential for northern areas.
Google Maps also has offline feature — download areas beforehand.

ELECTRICITY:
- Voltage: 220-240V, 50Hz
- Plug types: Type C and D (same as Europe)
- UK/US tourists: Bring adapter
- Power cuts (Load shedding): Still possible, especially in smaller cities
  Major hotels and restaurants have backup generators

SECTION 8: WEATHER AND BEST TIME TO VISIT
================================================================

NATIONWIDE BEST TIME: October to March
- Mild temperatures across the country
- All regions accessible
- Winter festivals and events

NORTHERN PAKISTAN (Hunza, Skardu, Gilgit, Swat):
- Best: May to October
- Peak: June-August (most roads open)
- Cherry Blossoms: Late March-April (Hunza most beautiful)
- Avoid: November-April (snow, road closures, extreme cold)

PUNJAB (Lahore, Multan, Faisalabad):
- Best: October to March
- Avoid: May-September (extreme heat, 40-48°C)
- Monsoon: July-August (humid, rain, can be beautiful)

KARACHI AND SINDH:
- Best: November to February
- Avoid: May-August (hot and humid)
- Monsoon: July-August (heavy rain, flooding risk)

ISLAMABAD:
- Best: March-April and October-November
- Spring and autumn most beautiful
- Summer acceptable (cooler than Lahore)

BALOCHISTAN (Quetta):
- Best: March to October
- Mild summers compared to rest of Pakistan
- Winter: Can snow, roads can close
