Travel Tips
🕐 Best Time to Visit
October to February offers pleasant 15–25°C weather. Avoid May–July — peak heat hits 45°C+.
🚖 Getting Around
Use Careem or inDrive for rides. The Orange Line Metro covers major city routes affordably.
🍽️ Food Tip
Head to Food Street (Fort Road) after sunset — best atmosphere and authentic Lahori cuisine.
💰 Budget Guide
Budget: ₨3,000–5,000/day. Mid-range: ₨8,000–15,000/day including meals and sightseeing.
